Common Questions

Everything You Need
To Know

How do I book an appointment?
You can book an appointment directly through our website using the quick booking bar or by calling our helpdesk. Our team will confirm your slot within minutes.
Do you accept health insurance?
Yes, we partner with major insurance providers globally. Please bring your insurance card during your visit for a seamless cashless experience.
Is emergency care available 24/7?
Absolutely. Our emergency department and trauma center are fully operational 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, with a dedicated team of specialists.
What should I bring for my first visit?
Please bring a valid ID, any previous medical records, and your current medications list to help our doctors understand your health history better.
