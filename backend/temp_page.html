About Us

Industrial Tube Corporation

Industrial Tube Corporation is a family owned and operated tube mill. We have been producing high quality brass, copper, phosphor bronze, and nickel silver tubing at our mill in Somerville, New Jersey for over twenty-five years; but our experience ranges for over a century. The current management at ITC is a fourth generation in a long history of quality tube making, dating back to the early 1900’s in Germany.
